A MORE CONSIDERED CYCLE

Four ways to make tech
work harder for longer.

01

Buy with purpose

Choose a device that meets your actual needs. Avoid upgrading simply because a newer model exists.

02

Care for your device

Use protective cases, keep software updated and follow safe charging and storage practices.

03

Repair and reuse

Where feasible, repair devices, replace components and pass working electronics on for reuse.

04

Recycle responsibly

Never mix electronic waste with ordinary household rubbish. Use authorised e-waste collection or take-back channels.
